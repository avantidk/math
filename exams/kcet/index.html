KCET: maths and previous-year questions

Karnataka's common entrance test for professional courses.

KCET, the Karnataka Common Entrance Test, is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for admission to professional courses in Karnataka. Mathematics is one of its subjects.

KCET at a glance

Full nameKarnataka Common Entrance Test
Conducted byKarnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
Held forAdmission to engineering, pharmacy, farm science and other professional courses in Karnataka
Who can appearStudents who have passed, or are appearing in, Class 12 (II PUC)
ModePen and paper (OMR answer sheet)
How oftenOnce a year
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology (engineering uses Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics)
Maths in the paperMultiple-choice questions based on the Class 11 and 12 (PUC) syllabus

Exam patterns change from year to year. Always check the official notification for the latest rules and dates.
