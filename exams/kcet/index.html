KCET: Everything you should know

Next KCET exam

KCET, the Karnataka Common Entrance Test, is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for admission to professional courses in Karnataka. Mathematics is one of its subjects.

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details
Exam Name & Full FormKCET — Karnataka Common Entrance Test (also called UGCET)
Conducting AuthorityKEA — Karnataka Examinations Authority (Government of Karnataka)

Minimum EligibilityPassed Class 12 / 2nd PUC with at least 45% aggregate in PCM/PCB (40% for Karnataka SC, ST, Category-1, and OBC 2A/2B/3A/3B candidates)

Section 2Question Paper Pattern & Number of Questions

KCET is conducted over two days in separate subject sessions. Every subject paper has 60 questions for 60 marks (+1 mark per right answer). Depending on your chosen degree stream, you write 3 or 4 papers.

Section 4Step-by-Step Timeline from Application to Admission

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1Online Registration (Jan–Feb)Fill out the UGCET form on the KEA portal, enter your 15- digit RD number for caste/income certificates, upload photo/signature, and pay the fee.
2Admit Card & Exam (April)Download the KCET Hall Ticket and write the offline OMR papers (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, and/or Biology) at your allotted exam centre.
3Result & Rank Card (May–June)KEA combines your Class 12 board marks and KCET scores (50:50 for Engineering/Agriculture) and publishes your category-wise rank.
4Document Verification (June– July)Verify your study certificates, marks cards, and reservation documents (online auto-verification via SATS/RD number or at KEA centres).
5Option Entry & Seat Allotment (July–Sept)Enter your preferred colleges and branches in priority order. KEA runs Mock, Round 1, Round 2, and Extended Round allotments.
6Choice Selection & ReportingSelect Choice 1 to freeze your seat, pay the KEA tuition fee online, download the Admission Order, and report to your allotted college.

Section 5Safe scores and 10-year marks vs rank

Admissions to prestigious engineering institutions in Karnataka (RVCE, BMSCE, MSRIT, PESIT) are computed using an exact 50:50 composite weightage: 50% from 2nd PUC / 12th Board PCM (out of 300) and 50% from the KCET Exam (180 marks: 60 Physics, 60 Chemistry, 60 Mathematics). Because board marks carry half the weight of the rank calculation, small variations in 2nd PUC PCM marks can shift a candidate's rank by several hundred positions at the top and several thousand positions in the mid-tier.

Section 6Year-Wise Registration, Attendance & Selection Numbers

In KCET, every student who scores valid marks receives a rank and is eligible for counselling. Actual seat selection depends on total available seats across Karnataka (approximately 86,000+ seats across Engineering, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Veterinary, and Nursing).

How "Selection" Works in KCET: Unlike pass/fail recruitment exams, KEA assigns a rank to almost all students who appear for the exam and submit their Class 12 marks. Around 85,000 to 90,000 students secure government-quota seats every year across all streams through KEA online counselling rounds.
