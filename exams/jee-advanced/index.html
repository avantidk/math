JEE Advanced: Everything you should know

JEE Advanced 2027 · Sunday, 23 May 2027

–days
–hours
–minutes
–seconds

Counting down to Sunday, 23 May 2027. Dates can change: confirm on the official website.

JEE Advanced is held for admission to the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs). It is conducted each year by one of the IITs on rotation, and candidates qualify through JEE Main. Its mathematics papers are known for multi-concept problems and a mix of question types.

Section 2Question Paper Pattern & Number of Questions

Unlike JEE Main, JEE Advanced does not fix the exact number of questions or total marks in advance— they vary slightly every year (typically 48 to 54 questions per paper, totaling 96 to 108 questions for 360 marks across both papers). Both papers test Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics equally.

Section 4Step-by-Step Timeline from JEE Main to IIT Admission

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1Qualify JEE Main (Jan–April)Rank within the top 2,50,000 candidates in JEE Main Paper 1 (meeting category-specific percentile cutoffs).
2JEE Advanced Registration (Late April)Register on jeeadv.ac.in using your JEE Main application number, upload documents, and pay the registration fee.
3Write Both Papers (May)Appear for both Paper 1 (3 hours) and Paper 2 (3 hours) on the same day. Missing either paper disqualifies you.
4Result & Rank List (June)Zonal IIT releases subject-wise and aggregate scores along with your Common Rank List (CRL) and Category Rank.
5JoSAA Choice Filling (June–July)Arrange your preferred IITs and branches in priority order on josaa.nic.in across 5–6 seat allotment rounds.
6Fee Payment & IIT Reporting (July)Pay the seat acceptance fee, complete online document verification, and report in person to your allotted IIT campus.

Section 5Safe scores and 10-year marks vs rank

Conducted for the top 2.5 lakh qualifiers of JEE Main. Maximum aggregate marks vary each year (historically between 360 and 396 marks across Paper 1 and Paper 2). Cutoffs and ranks are universally evaluated against aggregate percentage achieved. Unlike percentage-based board exams, JEE Advanced scoring features partial marking, integer-type numerical answers, and negative marking in select sections. Consequently, scores around 50%–60% place candidates in premier rank brackets, while scores above 70% correspond to top-150 ranks nationwide.
