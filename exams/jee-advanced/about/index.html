JEE Advanced: Complete Exam Guide

National Premier Engineering & Research Entrance Handbook

The Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced), popularly known as JEE Advanced, is the second and final stage of India's national engineering entrance system. It is the sole gateway for admission to undergraduate degrees (B.Tech, B.S., Dual Degree, Integrated M.Tech) across all 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) as well as premier research institutes like IISc Bangalore, IIST, IISERs, and RGIPT.

Exam Name & Full FormJEE Advanced — Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced)

FrequencyOnce a year (usually held on a Sunday in May)
Mandatory Two PapersPaper 1 (Morning: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM) and Paper 2 (Afternoon: 2:30 PM – 5:30 PM). Attempting both papers is compulsory to be considered for ranking.
Exam Mode & LanguagesOnline CBT (Computer-Based Test) in English and Hindi (students can switch language anytime during the exam)
Maximum AttemptsMaximum 2 attempts in 2 consecutive years (Class 12 appearing year + immediate next year)
Eligibility GatekeeperMust rank among the top 2,50,000 candidates in JEE Main Paper 1 (across categories) AND secure at least 75% aggregate in Class 12 boards (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or be in the top 20 percentile of their board.

Section 2Question Paper Pattern & Number of Questions

Unlike JEE Main, JEE Advanced does not fix the exact number of questions or total marks in advance— they vary slightly every year (typically 48 to 54 questions per paper, totaling 96 to 108 questions for 360 marks across both papers). Both papers test Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics equally.

Section 7Step-by-Step Timeline from JEE Main to IIT Admission

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1Qualify JEE Main (Jan–April)Rank within the top 2,50,000 candidates in JEE Main Paper 1 (meeting category-specific percentile cutoffs).
2JEE Advanced Registration (Late April)Register on jeeadv.ac.in using your JEE Main application number, upload documents, and pay the registration fee.
3Write Both Papers (May)Appear for both Paper 1 (3 hours) and Paper 2 (3 hours) on the same day. Missing either paper disqualifies you.
4Result & Rank List (June)Zonal IIT releases subject-wise and aggregate scores along with your Common Rank List (CRL) and Category Rank.
5JoSAA Choice Filling (June–July)Arrange your preferred IITs and branches in priority order on josaa.nic.in across 5–6 seat allotment rounds.
6Fee Payment & IIT Reporting (July)Pay the seat acceptance fee, complete online document verification, and report in person to your allotted IIT campus.

The figures in this guide are rounded or approximate and are compiled from the official notifications and public reports. They change from year to year, so always confirm the latest rules, dates and fees on the official website of the exam.
