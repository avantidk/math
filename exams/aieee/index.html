AIEEE: Everything you should know

AIEEE, the All India Engineering Entrance Examination, was conducted by the CBSE until 2012 for admission to NITs, IIITs and other institutions. From 2013 it was replaced by JEE Main.

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details (2002 – 2012 Era)
Exam Name & Full FormAIEEE — All India Engineering Entrance Examination
Conducting AuthorityCBSE — Central Board of Secondary Education (Ministry of HRD, Govt. of India)
Active Years & ReplacementConducted from 2002 to 2012 (11 editions). Replaced by JEE Main starting in 2013.
FrequencyOnce a year (held on the last Sunday of April / first Sunday of May)
Exam Mode & LanguagesOffline Pen-and-Paper (OMR) from 2002–2010; Hybrid (Offline + Optional Online CBT) introduced in 2011–2012. Conducted in English and Hindi (plus regional languages in select states).
Marking Scheme (Final Pattern)
  • +4 marks for every correct answer
  • −1 mark (1/4th) negative marking for every wrong answer
  • 0 marks for unattempted questions
Attempt Limit & AgeMaximum 3 consecutive attempts (Class 12 passing year + next 2 years); upper age limit was 25 years (30 for SC/ST/PwD).
Counselling BodyCCB — Central Counselling Board (later renamed CSAB / JoSAA)
