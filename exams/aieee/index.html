AIEEE: maths and previous-year questions

The all-India engineering entrance until 2012; replaced by JEE Main.

AIEEE, the All India Engineering Entrance Examination, was conducted by the CBSE until 2012 for admission to NITs, IIITs and other institutions. From 2013 it was replaced by JEE Main.

AIEEE at a glance

Full nameAll India Engineering Entrance Examination
Conducted byCentral Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)
Held forAdmission to B.E. / B.Tech and B.Arch at NITs, IIITs and other institutions
Who can appearStudents who had passed, or were appearing in, Class 12
ModeMainly pen and paper, with an online option in later years
How oftenOnce a year from 2002 to 2012. From 2013 it was replaced by JEE Main.
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Maths in the paperMultiple-choice questions on the Class 11 and 12 syllabus

Exam patterns change from year to year. Always check the official notification for the latest rules and dates.
