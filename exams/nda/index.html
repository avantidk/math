NDA: Everything you should know

The National Defence Academy (NDA) examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). Its Mathematics paper tests Class 11 and 12 topics, and this bank covers the questions of that paper.

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details
Exam Name & Full FormNDA & NA — National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination
Conducting AuthorityUPSC — Union Public Service Commission (Government of India)
Official Websitesupsc.gov.in & upsconline.nic.in (and joinindianarmy.nic.in for SSB call- ups)
FrequencyTwice a year — NDA-I (held in April) and NDA-II (held in September)
Exam Mode & LanguagesOffline (Pen-and-paper OMR test) printed bilingually in English and Hindi
Application Fee
  • ₹100 for General / OBC Male candidates
  • ₹0 (Exempted) for SC, ST, all Female candidates, and wards of JCOs/NCOs/ORs
Training Academies3 Years joint training at NDA, Khadakwasla (Pune) + 1 Year specialized pre-commission training at IMA Dehradun (Army), INA Ezhimala (Navy), or AFA Dundigal (Air Force)
Academic Degree AwardedB.Sc. / B.Sc. (Computer Science) / B.A. awarded by Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi (or B.Tech. for Navy & Air Force Technical cadets)

Section 2Eligibility Rules for Class 12 Students

CriteriaRequirement
Army Wing QualificationPassed or appearing in Class 12 (10+2) from any recognized board in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts). No minimum board percentage required.
Air Force, Naval Wing & INAPassed or appearing in Class 12 (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).
Class 11 StudentsNot eligible. Students currently studying in Class 12 (appearing candidates) can apply and write the exam.
CriteriaRequirement
Age Limit16.5 to 19.5 years at the start of the course (UPSC publishes exact Date of Birth brackets in every notification).
Gender & Marital StatusBoth unmarried male and unmarried female Indian citizens are eligible. Cadets cannot marry until full academy training is completed.
Number of AttemptsNo fixed limit — you can appear in every NDA-I and NDA-II cycle as long as your Date of Birth falls inside UPSC's age window.

Section 5Step-by-Step Timeline & Action Plan for Class 12 Students

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1UPSC Notification & Form (Dec / May)Apply on upsconline.nic.in (December for NDA-I in April; May for NDA-II in September). Select service preferences carefully.
2Write UPSC Exam (April / Sept)Attempt Maths (120 Qs in 150 mins) and GAT (150 Qs in 150 mins) on the same day. Focus on accuracy to avoid 1/3rd negative marks.
3Written Result & SSB Date SelectionWithin 3–4 weeks, UPSC publishes roll numbers of qualified candidates. Register on the service portal and pick your SSB interview date.
45-Day SSB & Medical BoardAttend SSB (travel allowance for AC-3 tier train is reimbursed for first-timers). If recommended, undergo a 5–7 day Military Hospital medical checkup.
5Final Merit List & Joining NDAUPSC combines Written + SSB scores (out of 1,800). Selected candidates receive joining instructions for NDA Khadakwasla (January or July term).

Section 6Safe scores and 10-year marks vs rank

Administered biannually by UPSC for entry into the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. Total evaluation spans 1,800 marks (Stage 1: Written Examination out of 900; Stage 2: SSB Interview out of 900). Note: NDA admissions observe strictly merit-based criteria with no caste-based reservation. Candidates must clear both sectional minimums in the written test and the overall written cutoff to be called for the 5-day Services Selection Board (SSB) assessment, followed by medical fitness clearance.

Section 7The 5-Day SSB Interview Process (900 Marks)

Candidates who clear the written exam are called to a Services Selection Board (SSB) centre (Allahabad, Bhopal, Bengaluru, Kapurthala, Varanasi, Mysuru, Gandhinagar, or Dehradun) for a 5-day personality assessment:

DayStage & TestsWhat Assessors Look For
Day 1Stage I — Screening Test (OIR + PP&DT)Verbal/Non-Verbal Intelligence Test + Picture Perception & Discussion Test. ~50–60% candidates are screened out on Day 1; the rest stay for Days 2–5.
Day 2Stage II — Psychological Tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SD)Story writing on 12 pictures (TAT), 60-word association (WAT), 60 real-life situations (SRT), and Self-Description (SD) tested by a Psychologist.
Days 3 & 4GTO Outdoor & Group TasksGroup Discussion, Group Planning Exercise (GPE), Progressive & Half Group Tasks, Individual Obstacles, Command Task, and 3-minute Lecturette.
Days 2–4Personal Interview (45–60 Mins)One-on-one rapid-fire and situational interview with the Interviewing Officer covering academics, family, hobbies, and current affairs.
Day 5Final Board ConferenceAll three assessors (Psychologist, GTO, Interviewing Officer) review your 15 Officer-Like Qualities (OLQs) and announce recommended candidates.

Air Force Flying Extra Test (CPSS): Candidates who opt for the Air Force Flying Branch and get recommended by SSB must also pass the Computerised Pilot Selection System (CPSS) and PABT (conducted only once in a lifetime).
