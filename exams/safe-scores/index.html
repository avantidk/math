Exam 1JEE Main

JEE Main is administered across multiple sessions out of 300 marks (75 questions of 4 marks each; 25 per subject). Normalized percentile scores map test-takers across shifts to determine eligibility for Top NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and eligibility for JEE Advanced. Because candidate volume has expanded from roughly 11–12 lakh test-takers in the late 2010s to over 14–15 lakh candidates in recent cycles, raw marks corresponding to a given percentile have risen in moderate- difficulty shifts. Top NIT admissions depend heavily on both All India Rank (AIR) and Home State versus Other State quotas.

Exam 2JEE Advanced

Conducted for the top 2.5 lakh qualifiers of JEE Main. Maximum aggregate marks vary each year (historically between 360 and 396 marks across Paper 1 and Paper 2). Cutoffs and ranks are universally evaluated against aggregate percentage achieved. Unlike percentage-based board exams, JEE Advanced scoring features partial marking, integer-type numerical answers, and negative marking in select sections. Consequently, scores around 50%–60% place candidates in premier rank brackets, while scores above 70% correspond to top-150 ranks nationwide.

Exam 3KCET

Admissions to prestigious engineering institutions in Karnataka (RVCE, BMSCE, MSRIT, PESIT) are computed using an exact 50:50 composite weightage: 50% from 2nd PUC / 12th Board PCM (out of 300) and 50% from the KCET Exam (180 marks: 60 Physics, 60 Chemistry, 60 Mathematics). Because board marks carry half the weight of the rank calculation, small variations in 2nd PUC PCM marks can shift a candidate's rank by several hundred positions at the top and several thousand positions in the mid-tier.

Exam 4IAT

Administered by the Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research for 5-Year BS-MS dual degrees across 7 IISERs and the 4-Year BS program at IISc Bangalore and IIT Madras (Medical Sciences). The examination evaluates 240 aggregate marks (60 each in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology). All four subjects carry equal weightage regardless of whether a candidate took PCM or PCB in Class 12, making cross-subject familiarity or strong accuracy in three subjects a common path to competitive scores.

Exam 5NDA

Administered biannually by UPSC for entry into the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. Total evaluation spans 1,800 marks (Stage 1: Written Examination out of 900; Stage 2: SSB Interview out of 900). Note: NDA admissions observe strictly merit-based criteria with no caste-based reservation. Candidates must clear both sectional minimums in the written test and the overall written cutoff to be called for the 5-day Services Selection Board (SSB) assessment, followed by medical fitness clearance.

Exam 6CUET-UG

Normalized scores determine admission to central institutions like the University of Delhi (DU), BHU, JNU, and Allahabad University. The primary competitive benchmark evaluates an 800-mark scale (1 Language + 3 Domain Subjects @ 200 marks each) for Commerce and Humanities, and a 600-mark PCM/PCB aggregate for Sciences. Because each subject is normalized across multiple shifts, raw scores can convert differently depending on shift difficulty; high percentiles in domain subjects are critical for top North Campus programmes.
