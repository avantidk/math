JEE Main: Complete Exam Guide

National Undergraduate Engineering & Architecture Handbook

The Joint Entrance Examination (Main), popularly known as JEE Main, is India's national-level entrance test for admission into NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and several state engineering universities. It also serves as the qualifying gateway for JEE Advanced (for admission to the 23 IITs).

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details
Exam Name & Full FormJEE Main — Joint Entrance Examination (Main)
Conducting AuthorityNTA — National Testing Agency (Ministry of Education, Govt. of India)
Official Websitejeemain.nta.nic.in & nta.ac.in
FrequencyTwice a year — Session 1 (January) and Session 2 (April). Best NTA score of the two sessions is used for ranking.
Exam ModeOnline CBT (Computer-Based Test), except B.Arch Drawing section which is offline pen-and-paper
Question Paper Languages13 Languages: English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu
Marking Scheme+4 marks for every correct answer; −1 mark negative marking for wrong answers; 0 marks for unattempted questions
Syllabus CoveredClass 11 and Class 12 NCERT Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics syllabus
Attempt Limit & AgeNo age limit. Candidates can appear for 3 consecutive years starting from their Class 12 passing year.
Class 12 Marks RequirementNo minimum percentage to write JEE Main; however, 75% aggregate in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST) or top 20 percentile is required for NIT/IIIT/GFTI admission.

Section 3Year-Wise Registration, Attendance & Selection Numbers

Section 7Step-by-Step Timeline from Application to Admission

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1Session 1 Registration (Oct–Nov)Register on jeemain.nta.nic.in, choose Paper 1 (B.Tech) or Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Plan), upload photo/signature, and pay the exam fee.
2Session 1 Exam & Result (Jan– Feb)Appear for the Computer-Based Test at your allotted centre. NTA releases your Session 1 NTA Percentile Score in February.
3Session 2 Exam & Final AIR (April)Optionally appear in Session 2 to improve your score. NTA picks the best of your two percentiles and publishes All India Ranks (AIR).
4JEE Advanced (May — Optional)If you rank in the top 2,50,000, register and appear for JEE Advanced for admission to the 23 IITs.
5JoSAA & CSAB Counselling (June–Aug)Fill your preferred NIT, IIIT, and GFTI choices on josaa.nic.in. Participate in 5–6 JoSAA rounds plus 2 CSAB special vacant- seat rounds.
6Seat Acceptance & ReportingPay the seat acceptance fee online, upload Class 12 marksheet & category certificates for verification, and report physically to your allotted institute.

Official websites: NTA JEE Main (official site) · JoSAA counselling
