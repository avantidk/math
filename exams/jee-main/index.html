JEE Main: Math PYQs, DPPs Analysis

Engineering entrance for NITs, IIITs and other institutes; the qualifier for JEE Advanced.

JEE Main is the national-level engineering entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is used for admission to NITs, IIITs and other centrally funded technical institutions, and its rank list decides who may appear for JEE Advanced. Mathematics is one of the three subjects.

JEE Main at a glance

Full nameJoint Entrance Examination (Main)
Conducted byNational Testing Agency (NTA)
Held forAdmission to B.E. / B.Tech at NITs, IIITs and other centrally funded institutions; also the qualifying round for JEE Advanced
Who can appearStudents who have passed, or are appearing in, Class 12
ModeComputer-based test
How oftenTwice a year (January and April sessions), held in several shifts
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Math in the paperMultiple-choice and numerical-answer questions. Recent shift papers have 25 math questions (20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical).

Exam patterns change from year to year. Always check the official notification for the latest rules and dates.
