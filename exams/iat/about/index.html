IAT: Complete Exam Guide

National Science & Research Undergraduate Entrance Handbook

The IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) is India's sole national entrance examination for admission into the 7 Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research (IISERs). It is also accepted for flagship undergraduate science and engineering degrees at IISc Bangalore, IIT Madras, IIT Guwahati, IACS Kolkata, and IIEST Shibpur.

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details
Exam Name & Full FormIAT — IISER Aptitude Test (Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research Aptitude Test)
Conducting AuthorityIISER Joint Admissions Committee (JAC) — Managed jointly by the 7 IISERs (one designated coordinating IISER each year)
Official Websiteiiseradmission.in
FrequencyOnce a year (usually held in June; registration opens in March)
Admission Channels100% through IAT only (KVPY and JEE Advanced admission channels to IISERs have been discontinued)
Exam Mode & LanguagesOnline CBT (Computer-Based Test) in English and Hindi across 730+ test centres in India
Marking Scheme
  • +4 marks for every correct answer
  • −1 mark negative marking for every wrong answer
  • 0 marks for unattempted questions
Maximum Attempts & AgeNo age limit. Maximum 3 consecutive attempts (Class 12 appearing year + next 2 years).
Class 12 EligibilityPassed Class 12 with Science stream (at least 3 subjects among Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology) and minimum 60% aggregate (55% for SC/ST/PwD).

Section 2Question Paper Pattern & Number of Questions

IAT consists of a single 3-hour paper containing 60 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) divided equally across all four science subjects. Even if you had PCM or PCB in Class 12, all 4 sections are open to attempt —you can answer questions from all 4 subjects to maximize your score out of 240.

Section 7Step-by-Step Timeline from Application to Admission

StepStageWhat the Student Must Do
1Online Registration (March–April)Register at iiseradmission.in, upload Class 10/12 details and category certificates (OBC-NCL/EWS issued on or after April 1), and pay the fee.
2Admit Card & IAT Exam (May– June)Download hall ticket and appear for the 3-hour Computer- Based Test (60 MCQs across Physics, Chemistry, Maths, and Biology).
3Class 12 Marks Upload & Result (June)Upload your final Class 12 marksheet on the portal (for appearing students). IISER JAC publishes the rank list based purely on IAT marks out of 240.
4Campus Preference Filling (July)Log in to the counselling portal and lock your preference order across all 7 IISERs and specialized BS/B.Tech programmes.
5Multi-Round Seat Allotment (July– Sept)IISER conducts 6 to 8 allotment rounds. Choose Accept & Float (for higher preference) or Accept & Freeze, pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF), and join campus in August.
