IIT-JEE: Everything you should know

IIT-JEE, the Joint Entrance Examination, was the entrance examination for the IITs until 2012. From 2013 it continued as JEE Advanced. These older papers remain valuable for concept-heavy practice.

Section 1Exam Overview & Basic Facts

ParticularOfficial Details (1961 – 2012 Era)
Exam Name & Full FormIIT-JEE — Indian Institute of Technology Joint Entrance Examination
Conducting AuthorityJoint Admission Board (JAB) — Conducted by one of the older zonal IITs each year on rotation (IIT Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Kanpur, Kharagpur, Madras, Roorkee)
Active Years & ReplacementConducted from 1961 to 2012 (52 years). Replaced by JEE Advanced starting in 2013.
FrequencyOnce a year (held on a Sunday in April; from 2000–2005 it had a Screening + Main two-stage format)
Exam Mode & LanguagesOffline Pen-and-Paper (Objective OMR sheets from 2006–2012; subjective descriptive papers prior to 2006). Printed in English and Hindi.
Two Mandatory PapersPaper 1 (3 Hours, Morning) + Paper 2 (3 Hours, Afternoon) on the same day. Both papers tested Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
Attempt Limit & Board RuleFrom 2006 onward: Maximum 2 consecutive attempts (Class 12 appearing year + next year) and minimum 60% aggregate in Class 12 boards (55% for SC/ST/PwD).

Section 2Question Paper Pattern & How IIT-JEE Evolved

IIT-JEE went through three distinct historical eras in how questions were asked—moving from 3-hour descriptive subjective papers to a two-stage Screening + Main exam, and finally to the fully objective 6- hour OMR format (2006–2012):

Historical Evolution of Exam Format (1961 – 2012)

Era / YearsExam StructureQuestion Style & Marking Details
1961 – 1999Single Stage (Subjective Papers)4 separate papers: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English (English was dropped later). Long step-by- step subjective derivations and numerical proofs graded manually by IIT professors.
2000 – 2005Two-Stage: Screening + MainScreening: 1 single 3-hour MCQ paper (PCM) to shortlist ~20,000 candidates. Main: 3 separate 2-hour subjective papers (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) for final rank.
2006 – 2012 (Final Era)Single Stage: Paper 1 + Paper 2 (Objective OMR)Two 3-hour objective OMR papers on the same day (6 hours total). Machine-graded optical bubble sheets with negative marking and partial marking.

Final IIT-JEE Objective Pattern (2010 – 2012 Editions)

PaperSubjects CoveredQuestions (2012)Marks (2012)Question Formats Included
Paper 1 (3 Hours)Physics (20 Qs) + Chemistry (20 Qs) + Maths (20 Qs)60 Qs210 MarksSingle-correct MCQs (+3/−1), Multiple- correct MCQs (+4/0), Integer-type 0–9 (+4/0)
Paper 2 (3 Hours)Physics (20 Qs) + Chemistry (20 Qs) + Maths (20 Qs)60 Qs198 MarksSingle-correct MCQs (+3/−1), Paragraph/Comprehension (+3/−1), Multiple-correct (+4/−1)

Transition from IIT-JEE to JEE Advanced (2013): Starting in 2013, direct registration for IIT-JEE ended. Instead, students first had to qualify in the top 1,50,000 (later raised to 2,50,000) of JEE Main to become eligible to write JEE Advanced for IIT admission.
