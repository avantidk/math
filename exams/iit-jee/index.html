IIT-JEE: maths and previous-year questions

The IIT entrance examination until 2012; continued as JEE Advanced.

IIT-JEE, the Joint Entrance Examination, was the entrance examination for the IITs until 2012. From 2013 it continued as JEE Advanced. These older papers remain valuable for concept-heavy practice.

IIT-JEE at a glance

Full nameJoint Entrance Examination for the IITs
Conducted byThe IITs (one IIT organised it each year)
Held forAdmission to the IITs
Who can appearStudents who had passed, or were appearing in, Class 12
ModePen and paper
How oftenOnce a year until 2012. From 2013 it continued as JEE Advanced.
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry and Mathematics
Maths in the paperQuestion styles changed over the years and included single-correct, multiple-correct, integer-answer and matching questions.

Exam patterns change from year to year. Always check the official notification for the latest rules and dates.
