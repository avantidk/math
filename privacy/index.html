Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information अंक-IT Mathematics Dashboard (“we”, “the website”) collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and the services behind it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email from Google sign-in, or your Student ID if you are an Avanti student, and whether you are a teacher or a student.
  • Profile details: school, class, stream, batch, category and gender, where you or your school give them. Teachers add these for their students.
  • Practice and test data: the tests you attempt, your answers, scores, time taken, ranks and reports, and worksheets that teachers save.
  • Payment records: for Premium, the order and payment reference, the amount and the status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay’s payment window and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Chat messages: messages that teachers post in the teachers’ chat.
  • Security information: the IP address and time of Student ID sign-in attempts, used to block guessing of IDs. For Student ID sign-in we check the date of birth you enter against the school record; we do not display it.
  • Browser storage: your sign-in session, your light or dark theme choice and a copy of question files so the site opens faster. These stay on your device and are cleared when you sign out where possible.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, give you the right plan and show your own results.
  • To let teachers see the results of students in their approved school, and to let Avanti administrators approve schools and manage accounts.
  • To take and confirm payments for Premium.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and answer your messages.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile, results and payments.
  • Teachers approved for a school can see the details and results of students of that school.
  • Administrators of the dashboard can see account details needed to run the service.
  • Other students and the public cannot see your results. Ranks compare scores without showing other students’ names.

Services we rely on

We use Google for sign-in, Supabase to store accounts and results and to serve the question files, Razorpay to process payments and GitHub Pages to host the website. These providers process data as needed to provide their service and under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies at the moment. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and results while your account is active, and payment records as long as needed for accounting. Sign-in attempt records are kept for a limited time for security.

Children

The dashboard is used by school students, many of them under 18. Students usually join through their school or teacher. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data, or ask what we hold about you, by writing to unkganit@gmail.com from the email or Student ID you use. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions? See the contact page.
