About us

अंक-IT Mathematics Dashboard exists to make serious JEE-level maths practice free to start and easy to follow, for every student and for the teachers who coach them.

  • 16,000+questions in the bank
  • 7,980Non-PYQ practice questions
  • 24 + 4chapters in the syllabus, plus out-of-syllabus
  • 7exams, with papers from 2000 to 2026

What you get

Question bank

More than 16,000 questions, filtered by chapter, topic, level, exam and year, each with its answer and a full solution.

Past papers, 2000 to 2026

JEE Main, JEE Advanced, KCET, NDA, IAT and the older IIT-JEE and AIEEE, shift by shift and chapter by chapter.

DPPs and Must Solve

Ready-to-print practice sheets, and hand-picked questions that keep coming back in JEE.

Handwritten handouts

Neat revision notes for every chapter of Class 11 and Class 12.

Online tests and reports

An exam-like screen with a timer and question palette, then your score, rank and weak topics.

Tools for teachers

Build a test, share one link, and follow every student of your school.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in free

    Use Google, or your Student ID if you are an Avanti student. Choose Student or Teacher once.

  2. 2

    Practise by chapter

    Open the question bank, DPPs, past papers and Must Solve, and check every answer against its full solution.

  3. 3

    Test and improve

    Take timed tests, read your report, and go back to the topics where marks were lost.

Who it is for

Students

Class 11, Class 12 and droppers preparing for JEE Main and JEE Advanced, and also KCET, NDA and IAT.

Teachers

Teachers who want ready questions, printable sheets and online tests for their classes, with results in one place.

Avanti Fellows learners

Students and teachers of Avanti Fellows get Premium free and sign in with their Student ID.

What we believe

Accuracy first

Every question has an answer and a worked solution. If something looks wrong, tell us and we correct it.

Clear, not clever

Solutions are written to be followed step by step, in the order a student would think.

Free to start

You can begin without paying. Premium adds extras such as PDFs, handouts and all-India ranks.
