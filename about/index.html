About us

JEE Maths practice for Class 11 and 12 students, and the teachers who coach them.

What we do

अंक-IT Mathematics Dashboard is a practice platform for JEE Maths. It brings a chapter-wise question bank with solutions, previous-year papers, daily practice problems (DPPs), Must Solve lists, revision handouts and NTA-style online tests into one place, for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, KCET, NDA and IAT preparation.

Who built it

The dashboard is built and maintained by Ankit Kamboj, a maths teacher, so that students can practise the questions that matter for each chapter and teachers can set tests and see results without extra tools.

How it works

  • Practise by chapter: filter questions by chapter, topic, level, exam and year, and see the solution.
  • Take tests: an exam-like screen with a timer, a question panel, mark for review and auto-submit, followed by a report with your score, accuracy and rank.
  • Teachers: build a test from the question bank, share one link with a class and see every student's result.

Free and Premium

You can start free by signing in with Google. Premium (₹299 a year for students, ₹599 for teachers) adds PDF downloads and printing, all-India ranks, comparison with all students and handouts. Students of Avanti Fellows get Premium free.
