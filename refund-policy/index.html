Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

No refund once paidWhen you buy a Premium membership and the payment goes through, the amount is not returned. Please decide before you pay.

What this means

  • Every payment for a plan on अंक-IT Mathematics Dashboard is final. We do not refund it, wholly or in part, whether you used the plan a lot, a little or not at all.
  • We also do not refund if you change your mind, buy the wrong plan, forget to use it, or lose access because you broke the terms of use.
  • A plan is not paused, cancelled early for money back, or moved to another person.

Why we work this way

A Premium membership gives you full access the moment it starts: the whole question bank, solutions, handouts and tests. Digital content cannot be handed back, so we keep the rule simple and the price low.

Try before you pay

The Free plan is open to everyone who signs in, and the previous-year questions stay free. Use it first, look at the plans and prices, and buy Premium only when you are sure it is what you want.

When a payment itself goes wrong

This is not a refund: it is fixing a mistake in the payment. If money left your account but your Premium did not start, write to us with the date, the amount and the payment reference you got from your bank or UPI app, and we will check it and set it right. Payments that fail are returned to your account by your bank or payment provider on their own schedule.

Contact

Questions about a payment? Write to unkganit@gmail.com or use the contact page. See also our terms of use and privacy policy.
