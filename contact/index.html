Quick answers

I cannot sign in. What should I check?

Make sure you picked the right option: Google for most users, and your Avanti ID with date of birth only if you are an Avanti student. If it still fails, use the Help & Support button in the Login window or write to us.

I paid for Premium but it is not showing.

Sign out and sign in again first. If it still shows Free, write to us with the payment date and the Razorpay payment reference so we can check it.

I chose the wrong role (Teacher or Student).

The role is saved the first time you sign in. Write to us from the email you used and we will help change it.

How do I delete my account?

Write to us from the email or Student ID you use, with the subject "Request about my personal data", and tell us you want your account deleted.

You can also open Help & Support in the Login window. Read how we handle your information in the privacy policy.
