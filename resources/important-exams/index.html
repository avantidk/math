Important Mathematics Exams After Class 12

Complete Career & Entrance Roadmap For Class 12 Maths Students

Master table-by-table guide to every major Mathematics-based entrance test across Pure Maths & Research, Top Engineering Institutes, Management, Architecture, Maritime, and Defence. Beyond JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NDA, IAT, KCET, and CUET-UG, having Mathematics in Class 12 unlocks India's highest-paying and most prestigious undergraduate degrees. Below is the complete, category-wise reference guide of all other important Mathematics exams you can write after Class 12.

Section 1Pure Mathematics, Statistics & Research Exams (Highest Prestige)

These exams lead to elite national research institutes with zero or minimal tuition fees, guaranteed monthly stipends, and top global placements in Quantitative Finance, Data Science, AI, and Academia.

Exam NameConducting Body & CollegesMaths Weightage & Question CountSeats, Fees & Stipend

  • Indian Statistical Institute
  • ISI Kolkata (B.Stat. Hons.)
  • ISI Bengaluru (B.Math. Hons.)
  • ISI Delhi (B.S. Data Science)
  • 100% Mathematics:
  • UGA (2 Hrs): 30 MCQs (+4 / −1)
  • UGB (2 Hrs): 8 Subjective proof-based Maths problems
  • ~120–140 seats total
  • ₹0 Tuition Fee
  • ₹5,000/month stipend + ₹5,000/yr book grant
CMI Entrance Exam
  • Chennai Mathematical Institute
  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Maths & Computer Science
  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Maths & Physics
  • 100% Mathematics:
  • Part A: Objective Maths MCQs/Short Answer
  • Part B: Subjective Olympiad-style proof problems (3.5 Hrs total)
  • ~60–75 seats
  • Full tuition waivers + ₹5,000/month fellowship for top students
NEST (National Entrance Screening Test)
  • NISER Bhubaneswar & UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai
  • 5-Year Integrated M.Sc. (Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Bio)
  • 25% Weightage (50 Marks):
  • 4 sections (PCMB) of 17 Qs each (50 marks each)
  • Best 3 sections counted (150 marks total)
  • ~257 seats (200 NISER + 57 CEBS)
  • ₹60,000/year DISHA scholarship + ₹20,000 summer internship grant

Smart Strategy for a Class 12 Maths Student: Preparing thoroughly for JEE Main + NCERT Class 11– 12 Mathematics automatically covers 85–90% of the syllabus for BITSAT, COMEDK, State CETs, IAT, NEST, CUET-UG, IMU CET, and Army/Navy TES. Add Olympiad-style proof practice only if targeting ISI Kolkata/Bengaluru or CMI Chennai.
